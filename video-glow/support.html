FAQ

Why is there no glow on Netflix and similar services?

Their video is protected (DRM), and the browser doesn’t let extensions read its frames, so the glow can’t follow the picture there.

How do I turn the glow off on one site?

Open the extension popup on that site and press “Turn off on …”. You can also enable separate settings for the site there.

What are the hotkeys?

Alt+Shift+B toggles the glow, Alt+Shift+G opens the floating settings panel. You can change them at chrome://extensions/shortcuts.

The glow is slow or uses a lot of CPU

Lower the blur and turn off “Light to the beat” if you don’t need it. If that doesn’t help, report a bug with the site and browser.

How do I restore the default settings?

The reset button at the bottom of the popup works on the second press. You can save a backup in the settings first.
