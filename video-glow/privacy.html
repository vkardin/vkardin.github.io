Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2026-10-25

Video Glow does not collect, store on a server, transmit, sell or share any personal data.

What the extension does with your data

  • It reads video frames from the page you are watching, entirely inside your browser, only to draw the glow. Frames are never saved, uploaded or sent anywhere.
  • Your settings (glow style, themes, per-site settings, list of disabled sites) are stored in your browser with chrome.storage. They are not sent to us.
  • There is no analytics, telemetry, advertising or remote code.
  • The "access to all sites" permission is used only to find <video> elements on pages and draw the glow next to them. No other page content is read.

Philips Hue (optional)

Only if you turn on Philips Hue: to find your Hue bridge the extension may ask Philips’ discovery service (discovery.meethue.com), and then it talks only to your bridge on your home network to send light colours. The bridge key is stored in your browser.

This website

This site uses no cookies, trackers or ads. Your chosen language is remembered only in your browser.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be published on this page with an updated date.
