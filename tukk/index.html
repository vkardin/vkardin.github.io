Five gestures, each with its own action

Every gesture gets its own action, sensitivity, cooldown and conditions.

  • Shake

    Shake the phone sharply twice.

  • Chop

    Two quick chopping motions, like Motorola's chop-chop.

  • Back tap

    Two or three taps on the back, calibrated for your phone and case.

  • Face down

    Turn the phone over onto its screen.

  • Volume buttons

    Press a volume button twice, or up then down. Needs optional accessibility access.

What a gesture can do

Repeat the gesture to switch back: shake again and the flashlight turns off.

  • SOS signal

Built to stay out of the way

Instant

Questions

Does it work with the screen off?

Yes. Each gesture can work with the screen on, off or both. With the screen off, sensors are read in batches to save battery.

Will it go off in my pocket?

Pocket detection, cooldowns and pauses during calls or charging keep accidental triggers away. Each gesture has a live test to tune its sensitivity.

Why would it ask for accessibility access?

Only for volume button gestures, screenshots and opening apps directly. It is optional, and Tukk never reads screen content.

My phone stops it in the background.

Settings, then Background work, has steps for Xiaomi, Samsung, Huawei, OnePlus and other phones.
