What happens when the power goes out

You do nothing. The mode turns on by itself, and the usual settings come back by themselves.

  • Cuts the draw

    Turbo boost off, efficient cores first, lower screen refresh rate and brightness, Bluetooth off, background services and VPN on pause.

  • Counts the time honestly

    The draw is measured on battery and remembered separately for work, films and games. The time left does not jump with every spike.

  • Knows your power bank

    Shows how much is left in it. Type the percent the bank shows, and the program learns how much it really gives.

  • Follows the schedule

    For Ukraine it reads the Koly Ye outage schedule: warns ahead, tops the battery up and switches back when the power returns.

  • Profiles for everything else

    Game, film, build, call, quiet. A program opens, its profile turns on. On the charger a game gets full power.

  • Keeps the laptop tidy

    Once a week it clears temporary files and caches, watches the battery wear and warns when something new adds itself to autostart.

One window where everything is visible

Draw, time left, battery and power bank, today's outages and the profile. The small panel by the tray shows the same in brief.

Questions

Does it break anything in Windows?

No. Before changing a setting the program remembers its value and puts it back when the mode turns off. If the laptop was switched off in the middle, everything is restored on the next start.

How much does it save?

It depends on the laptop. On mine the draw falls from 30–60 W to 12–17 W. The Statistics page has a button that measures it on yours: three minutes without saving, three with it.

I have no power bank. Is it still useful?

Yes. Everything except the power bank counter works from the laptop's own battery.

I am not in Ukraine. Will it work?

Yes. Only the outage schedule is tied to Ukraine; the mode itself turns on whenever the charger is unplugged, anywhere.

Why does Windows warn about the file?

The file is not signed with a paid certificate, so SmartScreen may ask on the first run. The code is open, and every release is built from it by GitHub.
