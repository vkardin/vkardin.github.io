Privacy policy

Last updated: November 1, 2026

In short

Video Drift processes everything locally in your browser. It does not collect, send or sell any data. There are no servers, analytics, ads or trackers.

What is stored and where

  • Settings, bookmarks and frame notes: browser sync storage (chrome.storage.sync). If you use browser sync, your browser vendor syncs them between your devices.
  • Viewing history, Watch later entries and in-page player geometry (including video titles, URLs, playback positions and last viewed times): local browser storage (chrome.storage.local) on this device only. You can disable recording in Settings.
  • JSON backups and Markdown exports are created on your device and saved only where you choose.

Permissions

  • Access to pages: needed to find the video on the page, float it, read the current time and show the "Continue from" prompt.
  • storage: keeps your settings, bookmarks and positions.
  • activeTab: lets the popup talk to the tab you are on.

Deleting your data

Delete bookmarks in the Bookmarks tab. Remove individual history entries or clear viewing history in the Continue tab. Disable history recording in Settings to stop saving new positions. Removing the extension deletes its stored data.
