FAQ

Why is sound not processed on Netflix and similar services?

Their video is protected (DRM), and the browser gives extensions silence instead of sound. Leveler detects this, keeps the original sound and says “This site does not support audio processing” in the popup. Speed control still works.

Will it ruin the sound?

No. Settings change smoothly without clicks, and a limiter at the output keeps the sound from clipping. The switch in the popup brings back the original instantly.

How is it better than the night mode on my TV?

It is basically the same idea, for the browser and with controls: pick a ready preset or tune the compressor and equalizer for your speakers or headphones.

Does it use a lot of CPU?

Hardly any. Processing runs on the browser’s built-in Web Audio, and the level meters are drawn only while the popup is open.

Why does it need access to all sites?

To find videos on the page and connect audio processing to them. Nothing is read or sent anywhere; everything happens on your device.
