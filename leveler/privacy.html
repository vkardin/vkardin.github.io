Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2026-10-26

Leveler processes everything locally on your device. It does not collect, store on a server, transmit, sell or share any personal data.

What the extension does with your data

  • It processes the sound of videos on the page you are watching (compressor, equalizer, gain) with the browser’s built-in Web Audio, entirely on your device. Audio is never recorded, saved, uploaded or sent anywhere.
  • Your settings (presets, per-site preset and speed, list of sites where audio processing is not supported) are stored in your browser with chrome.storage. They are not sent to us.
  • There is no analytics, telemetry, advertising or remote code.
  • The "access to all sites" permission is used only to find <video> and <audio> elements on pages, process their sound and change playback speed. No other page content is read.

Browser sync

Presets and per-site settings are kept in chrome.storage.sync. If sync is on in your browser, the browser itself (Google or Mozilla) copies them between your devices under its own privacy policy. Leveler has no server and never sees this data.

This website

This site uses no cookies, trackers or ads. Your chosen language is remembered only in your browser. If you send a message with the feedback form, it is delivered by FormSubmit.co to the developer’s email.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be published on this page with an updated date.
